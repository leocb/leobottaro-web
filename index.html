Apps

Products I have designed and built. Open the app, or open the source on GitHub.

Canal4

Venue broadcasts. Staff send a message from the web dashboard and it appears on the venue screens as a scrolling ticker.

  • Web dashboard and Electron display app.
  • Real-time over SpacetimeDB, with a channel per room.

React 19 · Electron · SpacetimeDB

Open Canal4

Gabaritero

Answer-sheet grading, in beta. Offline-first grader and test generator for teachers.

  • Scan with the phone camera and mark answers on screen.
  • Grades and reports stay available without a connection.

React · TypeScript · Supabase · OpenCV.js

Open Gabaritero

LS9 Remote

Mixer control for Android. Android control for the Yamaha LS9 digital mixer, built for live events and church services.

  • Faders, EQ, dynamics and effects from the phone.
  • Scene recall and a full offline demo.

Java · Android · EtherMIDI · SysEx
